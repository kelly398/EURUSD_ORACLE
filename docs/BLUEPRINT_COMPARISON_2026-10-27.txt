EUR/USD ORACLE: original blueprint versus current implementation
Review date: October 27, 2026

ASSESSMENT

The implementation is more rigorous than the seven-page Version 1.1 blueprint
about data contracts, causal timestamps, model compatibility, source provenance,
and explicit failure states. It is less complete than the blueprint's intended
end-to-end autonomous, continuously learning operation. These are different
dimensions: stronger engineering does not establish stronger trading returns.

All 31 seats now have implementations in the canonical registration and runner
path. That milestone does not mean 31 independent learned predictors, 31 admitted
production sources, or a completed hedge-fund operating system. The next major
milestone should be a measurable prediction/outcome feedback loop and reliable
shadow operation, rather than adding seats.

BLUEPRINT COVERAGE

Data intelligence / market state:
  Substantially implemented: PostgreSQL, quote ticks, broker candles, freshness
  and quality checks, causal snapshots, trend, volatility, momentum, structure,
  liquidity and timezone-aware sessions. Earlier read-only inspection found
  roughly 30.7 million ticks and 75,528 canonical M5 broker candles. However,
  the latest stored tick/candle evidence was October 7. Live MT5 checks failed
  during the earlier audit. Current operation is therefore not established.

Council of 31:
  Implemented as a canonical catalogue, staged runner and typed context. Primary
  opinions precede governance, which precedes synthesis. Registration, missing
  inputs, source admission and execution are distinct. The live context factory
  still does not load COT by default, despite the COT agent/repository existing.
  Supplemental delivery is an extension point, not a running collector service.

Consensus:
  An explicit initial implementation exists, with supplied reliability and regime
  weights, category balancing, minimum support and independent governance vetoes.
  Historical reliability is not yet learned and maintained by an outcome service.
  Category balancing and confidence comparability require further research.

MiroFish / scenario engine:
  No separate implementation was found. Seat 10 performs a seeded empirical
  bootstrap: 5,000 paths over three M5 bars, sampled independently from recent
  returns. That is a useful small simulation component, but does not implement
  the blueprint's separate scenario environment. It does not model a complete
  strategy, regime transitions, spread evolution, correlated return blocks,
  execution uncertainty or event-conditioned shocks. The PDF supplies a name and
  high-level purpose, rather than a precise MiroFish integration specification.

Risk governance:
  Deterministic veto checks exist for supplied portfolio state, exposure, proposed
  risk, drawdown, freshness, spread, ATR and event coverage. Position sizing,
  broker reconciliation, order lifecycle and an independent execution-boundary
  risk check are not complete. Fractional Kelly is not implemented. It should
  remain a later sizing experiment requiring validated net-return distributions;
  agent confidence cannot be substituted directly for a Kelly probability.

Execution / paper trading:
  No complete broker order/position management engine or paper trading lifecycle
  was found. Council execution_authorized is deliberately always false. Research
  inference and JSON output are not paper trading or a live capital allocator.

Learning memory / database:
  Raw inputs, macro data and ML training samples are persisted. No persistent
  Council decision/agent prediction/outcome/performance ledger was found in the
  inspected application schema. The blueprint's market_state, agents,
  agent_predictions, simulations, trades and agent_performance roles are not all
  implemented as persisted operating records. Agents may appropriately remain a
  versioned code catalogue, but decision provenance and outcome memory must exist
  somewhere durable. A context hash alone does not preserve a replayable decision.

Five-minute cycle / monitoring:
  The supervisor manages tick and M5 bar ingestion workers. It does not schedule
  a durable Council -> proposal -> risk -> execution/paper -> outcome cycle.
  backend/main.py prints a startup banner; it does not operate the entire system.
  No application dashboard frontend was found. Restart supervision exists, but
  cycle deadlines, durable retries, health telemetry, missed-cycle recovery,
  alerting and complete operational observability remain additional work.

Economic validation:
  Chronological data partitions, label-horizon purging, causal replay, frozen
  model evaluation and neural calibration are real strengths. They do not yet
  constitute a costed Council-level backtest, walk-forward evaluation of adaptive
  weights, execution stress test or prospective paper trading record.

SPECIFIC FINDINGS THAT MATTER FOR ADAPTATION

1. Pattern Recognition does not fulfill the original historical-similarity role.
   backend/agents/implementations/pattern_recognition_agent.py checks the last two
   candles for bullish/bearish engulfing and pin bars. Scores such as 0.80 and
   0.55 are fixed; confidence follows score magnitude. It has no historical
   retrieval store, learned pattern success rates, regime-conditioned outcomes,
   or updating memory. It is an interpretable candlestick heuristic.

   There is some analogue reasoning elsewhere: StatisticalProbabilityAgent
   compares fast/slow move states within a recent 180-candle window. TimeSeries
   refits an AR(1)-style relationship over recent returns. These respond to new
   observations, but are not the persistent, evaluated learning system envisioned.

2. Learning currently occurs mainly offline.
   XGBoost and the three-member residual neural network load saved trained
   artifacts. The neural input is one 27-feature row, not a recurrent candle
   sequence. This architecture is not inherently wrong; a sequence model should
   replace it only if controlled evaluation demonstrates incremental value.
   HMM posterior state changes as new observations arrive, using frozen model
   parameters. Regime inference and parameter learning are different capabilities.
   No automatic mature-outcome -> reliability -> retraining -> promotion/rollback
   operating loop was found.

3. Regime information is not yet fully connected to adaptive governance.
   HMM state probabilities, switch probability and entropy are contextual output.
   Synthesis consumes caller-supplied regime_compatibility numbers; it does not
   derive empirical compatibility from matured agent results in each HMM state.
   Risk Officer does not directly turn HMM uncertainty or anomaly evidence into
   a learned risk policy. Contextual data should influence justified constraints
   and suitability without being forced into an invented directional vote.

4. The recent category weighting deserves revision before production use.
   chief_strategist_agent.py first computes each category's weighted mean and then
   averages category scores equally. This limits category headcount dominance,
   but cancels the absolute reliability scale within a category. A lone agent
   with score -0.8 and reliability 0.01 gives its category -0.8, just as one with
   reliability 0.99 does. An opposite +0.8 category still cancels it equally.
   This was reproduced with the exact arithmetic used by the implementation.

   This is a limitation of code introduced during our recent work, not a claim
   that the existing implementation is seamless. A researched hierarchical
   weighting policy should account for category evidence strength, uncertainty
   and dependence, while preserving concentration limits. More category labels
   do not prove independent evidence.

5. Directional votes lack a common prediction target contract.
   AgentOpinion records analysis time, direction, score and confidence, but no
   explicit forecast horizon, target definition or cost assumption. TimeSeries
   describes a next-M5-return forecast; Monte Carlo uses three bars; several
   technical seats describe current conditions rather than an evaluated future
   event. Averaging these scores does not itself make them comparable forecasts.
   Introduce versioned forecast specifications and evaluate each seat against
   its declared target. Retain separate contextual roles and confidence meanings.

6. Many confidence values are signal-strength heuristics.
   Several technical agents set confidence to abs(score). A 0.8 engulfing score
   is not demonstrated to mean an 80% correct forecast. HMM posterior certainty
   concerns latent state assignment, not trade profitability. Neural temperature
   calibration already exists and should be preserved; the problem is the
   comparability of all Council confidence fields and the absence of longitudinal
   calibration for the whole ensemble. Averaged confidence is not automatically
   a calibrated ensemble probability.

7. Apparent diversity needs empirical measurement.
   Many agents reuse the same candles, market-state features and quote-flow
   engine. The ML models also share the same 27-feature schema. This is valid
   modular design, but 20 agreeing transformations of one move are not 20
   independent confirmations. Measure residual/error dependence and incremental
   net value through chronological ablation. Do not infer independence from names.

8. Risk checks need a concrete proposal to protect.
   PortfolioRiskSnapshot supplies aggregate proposed risk/exposure amounts, but
   there is no complete immutable trade intent tying direction, instrument,
   quantity, entry, stop, account conversion, costs and expiry to those amounts.
   Governance currently precedes final synthesis. A later execution design must
   challenge and recheck the exact proposal after synthesis and immediately
   before any order, including concurrent reservations and reconciliation.

9. A stalled agent can stall the cycle.
   CouncilRunner uses ThreadPoolExecutor/as_completed without a cycle deadline.
   Exception isolation exists, but there is no hard containment of a hanging
   task. A timeout on future.result alone would not stop a running thread or
   avoid executor shutdown waiting. Bounded worker processes or another real
   isolation mechanism need evaluation for the operational runner.

10. Production data delivery is still incomplete.
   A working FRED key and successful SP500/VIXCLS capture establish access to a
   daily cross-asset proxy. They do not establish intraday sentiment, event
   consensus forecasts, complete EUR/USD calendar coverage or Fed historical
   source admission. Production Fed/rate/calendar gates remain closed. Today's
   downloaded values cannot silently become historical as-of observations.

11. Reproducibility and maintenance need finishing.
   requirements-lock.txt omits ML dependencies declared in requirements.txt,
   including scikit-learn, xgboost, joblib and torch. The running environment is
   not yet fully reconstructible from that lock file. Earlier audit also found
   training-index schema drift, manifest accounting that needs reconciliation,
   and a recursive error-helper branch in audit_ml_dataset.py. The current
   version-filtered query makes that branch unreachable during the passing audit;
   it remains a defect worth correcting in a separate maintenance change.
   Current MT5 boundary guards also do not certify the entire historical archive.

WHAT THE SAVED MODEL RESULTS ACTUALLY SHOW

Read existing evaluation.json files under backend/models/registry; did not rerun
or select models against their held-out data.

Fixed test samples:                 10,817
Always predict test majority class: 35.70% accuracy (descriptive reference only)
XGBoost:                           42.18% accuracy; macro F1 about 0.416
Calibrated neural ensemble:         42.35% accuracy; macro F1 about 0.416
Neural improvement in accuracy:      0.17 percentage points over XGBoost

These are three-class direction/neutral classification results, not trade win
rates or cost-adjusted returns. The neural ensemble is not shown to be materially
superior to XGBoost by this small accuracy difference alone. The saved calibrated
neural >=0.60 confidence slice contains only 99 observations, about 0.92% coverage,
with 75.76% accuracy. It is too limited to justify a general high-win-rate claim or
choosing a fresh threshold on the consumed test. Dependence and overlapping
forecast horizons further limit treating observations as independent evidence.

The HMM was accepted as contextual and remains non-directional. Its posterior
certainty must not be presented as 90% confidence that a trade will win.
The existing training dataset has 72,363 samples over roughly one year. Millions
of raw ticks do not provide millions of independent, labelled market episodes.

RECOMMENDED NEXT DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION ORDER

A. Restore trustworthy operation and preserve the current baseline.
   Resolve MT5 connectivity and timestamp-domain evidence; verify fresh closed
   M5 bars and raw ticks. Complete the dependency lock and checkpoint the current
   changes. Use concrete source-delivery acceptance tests rather than additional
   layers of admission machinery without a delivery milestone. Preserve valid
   gates and the frozen v1 artifacts.

B. Build decision memory before automatic learning.
   Persist each cycle, source/version identities, input snapshot references,
   per-agent outputs/blockers, model/policy versions, proposed action and vetoes.
   Assign stable cycle/prediction IDs with uniqueness constraints. After each
   forecast horizon matures, attach an immutable outcome using correct bid/ask
   and cost assumptions. Record abstentions and rejected proposals too, while
   distinguishing hypothetical outcomes from actual fills. Recovery must neither
   double-score outcomes nor silently rewrite earlier decisions.

C. Establish an economic evaluation harness.
   Replay baseline agents and Council policies chronologically with consistent
   forecast/position rules, spread, commissions, slippage, latency and financing
   where applicable. Measure calibration, coverage, net expectancy, drawdown and
   regime/session stability, including uncertainty. Compare single-agent and
   simple-policy baselines with the full Council. Use walk-forward evaluation,
   label-horizon purging and explicit tracking of research trials. Already-used
   test data becomes historical evidence; v2 needs a new evaluation protocol and
   fresh prospective holdout, not another supposedly untouched run of v1.

D. Add bounded adaptive weighting.
   Derive time-decayed skill estimates from matured forecasts, conditional on
   session, regime and horizon. Shrink sparse estimates toward conservative
   priors and require sufficient evidence. Initially adapt suitability and
   abstention before position size. Track both input distribution changes and
   delayed prediction-loss changes: a new market distribution is not by itself
   proof that a model has failed. Evaluate drift detectors as monitoring tools,
   with explicit false-alert and trading-calendar treatment.

E. Make historical pattern recognition real.
   Keep named candlestick patterns as explainable baseline features. Add a
   versioned memory of normalized historical windows: returns, candle geometry,
   ATR, session, quote activity and spread conditions. Retrieve similar earlier
   windows whose outcomes were already available at decision time. Exclude
   overlapping/self matches and enforce distance/sample support requirements.
   Return outcome distributions, adverse/favorable excursion, costs and
   uncertainty, not a single attractive chart match. Abstain on unfamiliar
   conditions. Start with a transparent nearest-neighbor baseline; compare
   matrix-profile motifs and later learned representations on fresh evaluations.
   A similar-looking path is a retrieval result, not proof of a predictive edge.

F. Separate model experimentation from live promotion.
   Drift or a scheduled trigger may launch an offline challenger. Evaluate it
   against the frozen champion on chronological data, then run it in shadow.
   Require recorded promotion criteria, artifact hashes and rollback support.
   Online weighting and retraining are distinct decisions. Any future automatic
   promotion authority should have explicit limits. Learned components must not
   change source admission, financial limits or execution permissions themselves.

G. Implement scenarios and paper operation before capital deployment.
   Begin with empirically validated regime-conditioned/block-bootstrap paths and
   adverse spread/gap/event stress scenarios. Check scenario calibration and
   incremental decision value. A complex simulator is useful only if this
   evidence supports it. Then build an idempotent M5 shadow scheduler, concrete
   trade intents, persistent paper order/position state, reconciliation, deadlines,
   health monitoring and a dashboard. Test restarts, duplicate events, stale
   inputs and disconnections. Capital deployment remains a later separate step.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA FOR THE ADAPTIVE MILESTONE

  - Every evaluated forecast is traceable to its exact source/model/policy version.
  - Outcomes become visible only after their declared maturity time.
  - Replays reproduce decisions without future information.
  - Adaptive weights demonstrate benefit over frozen and simple baselines across
    successive unseen windows, after realistic costs and with uncertainty shown.
  - Pattern retrieval excludes self/overlapping/future matches and abstains when
    support is weak; selected examples do not substitute for aggregate evaluation.
  - Missing/stale data, deadlines, restarts and duplicate deliveries have tested,
    deterministic recovery or HOLD behavior.
  - Risk enforcement acts on the exact proposed order and remains independent.
  - Frozen artifacts, immutable outcomes and rollback keep experiments auditable.
